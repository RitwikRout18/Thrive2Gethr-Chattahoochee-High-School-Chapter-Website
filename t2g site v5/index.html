Who We Are

At Thrive2Gethr, we are a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering the next generation to take action against food insecurity. By standing together—connecting donors, passionate volunteers, and local families—we step up to transform empty plates into nourishing, vital meals.

With every partnership formed and every resource shared, we build a resilient future where everyone thrives—together.

Our Mission

As a proud chapter of the national Thrive2Gethr movement, our mission is to empower every student to thrive mentally, emotionally, and socially. It also empowers you to lead initiatives that combat food insecurity and reduce food waste in your community.

Make a Real Impact

Volunteering with Hooch Thrive2Gethr isn't just about logging hours—it's about making a profound difference in your local community. Each student has the power to tackle food waste and support families in need.
